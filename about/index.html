01 · About

There are two of me.

Entrepreneur, engineer and identical twin, born in Barcelona in 1997. The short version: the people, the habits and the obsessions behind everything I build.

  1. 01

    Alberto, my twin

    Alberto is my identical twin. My co-founder, my training partner, my rival and my best friend, all in one person. Every company I have ever started, I started with him.

    He is the CEO: sales, fundraising, hiring, everything outside the building. I am the CTO: AI models, infrastructure and product, everything inside it. There are no politics between us, because there can't be. Building with him is the purest form of love I know.

    His side ↗Rosas Twins on YouTube ↗
  2. 02

    Four of us

    Alberto is not the only one. I have two sisters, Alba and Andrea. Alba grew up with us, and Andrea came 15 years later. Four kids, one family, and everything I build, I build with them in mind.

  3. 03

    Building is my art

    Some people paint. Some people write songs. I build companies. A company starts as nothing: a blank page, two brothers and a problem. Then it becomes a place where people do the best work of their lives, and something strangers rely on every day.

    It is also how I express love: for my family, for the people who give us years of their lives, and for people I will never meet. Some people say it. I ship it.

  4. 04

    Sport since five

    Tennis since I was five, at 7am every day. No secret, just consistency. It took me to the US on a scholarship at New Mexico State, and I still play. Then came boxing, skydiving and wingsuit, for the same reason as everything else: I want to see it myself.

  5. 05

    A clear head

    Not one sip of alcohol since COVID. I still dance to techno until five in the morning, stone sober. Black every day, for years.

  6. 06

    My people, and nature

    I love my people. You are the average of your five closest friends, and I got lucky with mine: same table, same dance floor, same bad ideas, for years. And when I need to feel small, I go to the sea and the mountains, and I photograph them.

Just getting started.
