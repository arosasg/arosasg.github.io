01 · About

There are two of me.

Entrepreneur, engineer and identical twin, born in Barcelona in 1997. The short version: the people, the habits and the obsessions behind everything I build.

01

Alberto, my twin

My co-founder, my training partner, my rival and my best friend, all in one person. Every company I have started, I started with him. He is the CEO, everything outside the building. I am the CTO, everything inside it. Building with him is the purest form of love I know.

02

Four of us

Alberto is not the only one. I have two sisters, Alba and Andrea. Alba grew up with us, and Andrea came 15 years later. Four kids, one family, and everything I build, I build with them in mind.

03

Building is my art

Some people paint. Some people write songs. I build companies. A company starts as nothing: a blank page, two brothers and a problem. Then it becomes something strangers rely on every day. Some people say they love. I ship it.

04

Sport since five

Tennis since I was five, at 7am every day. No secret, just consistency. It took me to the US on a scholarship at New Mexico State, and I still play. Then came boxing, skydiving and wingsuit: I want to see it all myself.

05

A clear head

Not one sip of alcohol since COVID.

I still dance to techno until five in the morning, stone sober. Black every day, for years.

06

My people, and nature

I love my people. You are the average of your five closest friends, and I got lucky with mine. And when I need to feel small, I go to the sea and the mountains, and I photograph them.
