03 · Vision

The biggest secrets are still out there.

We have set foot on one moon. The rest of the galaxy is still waiting. I want to see humanity out there, exploring it, and I want us to answer the biggest questions there are.

  • What is the universe made of?
  • How did it begin?
  • What is consciousness?
  • Are we alone?
  • How far can we go?
  1. 01

    Why I build

    A future like that does not arrive by itself. It gets built by people who refuse to stop, one company at a time. Every company puts more people, more tools and more intelligence on hard problems. Didit, Santé and everything that comes after them are practice for that.

  2. 02

    Intelligence, pointed at the stars

    I build AI every day. I think it will be the biggest lever humanity has ever held, and I want it pointed at the hardest questions we have, out there and in here.

  3. 03

    Beyond my lifespan

    There is no greater purpose than shaping a better life, not only for yourself, but for those who will walk this earth long after you're gone.
