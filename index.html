01 · Art

Building companiesis my art.

Some people paint. Some people write songs. I build companies.

A company starts as nothing: a blank page, two brothers and a problem. Then it becomes a place where people do the best work of their lives, and something strangers rely on every day. That is the most beautiful thing I know how to make.

It's how Iexpress love.

I build with my brother. I build for my sisters, Alba and Andrea. I build for the people who give us years of their lives, and for people I will never meet. Every late night is a way of saying I care about this, about you, about what comes after us.

Some people say it. I ship it.

There is no greater purpose than shaping a better life, not only for yourself, but for those who will walk this earth long after you're gone.

Creation, especially when done as a team, transforms you. It pushes you to grow, to understand others, and to connect more profoundly with the universe itself.

Gamium

The one that failed

It started with GPU farms mining Ethereum. Then came the biggest idea we could find: a metaverse where your avatar was your identity and your data belonged to you. We were 24.

~€3MRaised
€1MOf virtual land sold in 10 days
33People at the peak

We had press, investors and 33 people. We did not have product-market fit. It failed. Best education I ever paid for.

Make mistakes and learn from them. Think about life beyond your lifespan. You are here now to build a better future for tomorrow. Take risks and give your best!

Me, June 2022, in the middle of it

07 · Cosmos

The biggest secrets are still out there.

We have set foot on one moon. The rest of the galaxy is still waiting. I want to see humanity out there, exploring it, and I want us to answer the biggest questions there are.

  • What is the universe made of?
  • How did it begin?
  • What is consciousness?
  • Are we alone?
  • How far can we go?

A future like that gets built by people who refuse to stop. That's the work. Everything on this page is practice.

Dreaming higher than ever, not even death can stop a passionate soul in search of the light.
